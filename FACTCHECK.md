# Fact-check report: Samvidhan Explorer commentary

**Date:** November 2026
**Scope:** all AI-drafted commentary: 507 explanations (the Preamble and 506 articles), 106 amendment entries plus pending Bills, and 68 landmark-judgment notes.
**Not in scope:** the verbatim constitutional text. That is the official Legislative Department edition as on 1 May 2024, and the app shows it unaltered.

## Method
- Nine checking passes were run in parallel. Each one verified specific claims against web sources and against the official text and its footnotes. Claims checked were dates, citations, bench sizes, majority splits, holdings, amendment provenance and "current status" lines as of November 2026.
- Each pass fixed only factual errors, misleading simplifications and outdated statements. Every change was logged; the logs follow this summary.
- Every case and amendment entry now carries `sources` (1–3 URLs) and `checked: "2026-11"`. The app shows these as a **Sources** panel with a "Fact-checked" badge.

## Most significant corrections
- **Delhi services law (Art. 239AA, case note nct-delhi-2023):** the notes said the challenge to the GNCTD (Amendment) Act 2023 was pending. The petitions were in fact withdrawn on 23 May 2025.
- **106th Amendment (women's reservation):** commenced on **16 April 2026** (S.O. 1922(E)). Seats are still subject to post-census delimitation. Arts. 330A and 334A and the amendment entry were updated, and an editorial "update since this text edition" note now appears on Arts. 239AA, 330A, 332A and 334A.
- **Supreme Court strength (Art. 124):** now 37 judges plus the CJI (38 in total), under the Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Amendment Act 2026. It received assent on 11 August 2026 and is deemed in force from 16 May 2026.
- **Uniform Civil Code (Art. 44):** Uttarakhand's law has been in force since January 2025. Gujarat (March 2026) and Assam (May 2026) have passed UCC bills.
- **Sajjan Singh (1964):** the outcome was unanimous. The 3:2 split was over reasoning, not result.
- **ADM Jabalpur:** the related amendment is the 38th, not the 42nd.
- **32nd Amendment:** the summary misdescribed it. It omitted Art. 371(1).
- **7th and 44th Amendments:** lists of provisions affected corrected. The 44th did not amend Art. 366 or Art. 368; the Rajya Sabha rejected those clauses.
- **Art. 356, 360 and 371:** amendment history corrected. For example, the 44th Amendment *substituted* Art. 356(5); it did not add it.
- **Art. 301:** the Atiabari (1961) and Automobile Transport (1962) holdings were wrongly merged into one; they are now separated.
- **Art. 244A:** the power *was* used once, to create autonomous Meghalaya in 1970.
- **No new amendment Act after the 106th:** none was enacted up to November 2026. The Constitution (131st Amendment) Bill 2026, which would have enlarged the Lok Sabha, was defeated on 17 April 2026. The 129th Bill (simultaneous elections) and the 130th Bill (removal of ministers in custody) are still before Joint Parliamentary Committees.

## Coordinator checks (beyond the nine passes)
- **Integrity:** three passes edited `judgments.js` at the same time. All 20 logged corrections were confirmed present in the final files, and every case and amendment entry is marked checked.
- **Contradiction resolved from the primary source:** the passes disagreed on whether Part IXB still applies to co-operatives confined to a single Union Territory (*Union of India v. Rajendra N. Shah*, 2021). The official judgment, para 78, says that for co-operative societies "which have no ramifications outside the Union territory itself, Part IXB will have no application". The case note was corrected; Art. 243ZS was already right.
- **Independent re-verification of post-2025 claims:** the 106th Amendment's commencement, the 38-judge Supreme Court, the Gujarat and Assam UCC bills, and the November 2025 Presidential Reference were each confirmed in separate searches.
- **Source quality:** 46 links to exam-prep sites, AI tools and document-sharing sites were removed. Ten entries left with no source were given primary or legal-press sources (Indian Kanoon, the Supreme Court Digital SCR, SC Observer, Bar & Bench, LiveLaw). About 110 links still point to Wikipedia, mainly the amendments list, and about 50 to legislative.gov.in. Replacing the Wikipedia links with the Acts' gazette texts is a worthwhile follow-up.
- **Art. 256:** the verbatim text ends with a colon instead of a full stop. That is how the official 2024 PDF prints it, so it was left as is.

## Open items for an expert reviewer
- **Sabarimala reference:** a nine-judge bench reserved judgment on 14 May 2026, and no verdict had been reported by November 2026. This entry is still flagged `verify`.
- **Pending matters to keep watching:** the challenge to the Chief Election Commissioner and Election Commissioners Act 2023 (a split two-judge bench in September 2026 sent it to the CJI for a larger bench); the Money Bill reference (Rojer Mathew); the Nabam Rebia reference; and Kerala's borrowing suit (Art. 293).
- **Citations:** these are blank where no reliable source confirmed them: Rajendra N. Shah, Mohit Minerals, Subhash Desai.
- **Minor date ambiguities:** Hussainara Khatoon (12 vs 26 Feb 1979) and the assent dates of the 70th and 85th Amendments.
- **Art. 334A:** it is unclear whether the "fifteen years" runs from the 16 April 2026 commencement or from when the reservation takes effect.
- **7th Amendment:** its list of articles affected is representative, not exhaustive.
